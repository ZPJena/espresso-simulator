Local Risks & Bottleneck Finders

In chemical engineering, an exothermic tubular reactor can develop a localized hot spot due to high reaction rates and poor heat extraction. If unchecked, this leads to thermal runaway.

Similarly, a gaming smartphone is an assembly of highly conducting and insulating layers. When the CPU runs at maximum load, it generates heat. If a bottleneck exists (like degraded thermal paste or hand blocking convective cooling), heat is trapped locally.

When the CPU temperature crosses \(80^\circ\text{C}\), safety algorithms trigger thermal throttling to prevent hardware destruction. Designing thermal bridges keeps local temperatures safe.
