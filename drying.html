📐 Theory & Governing Equations

Bulk moisture flux Jbulk = −Deff ∇w

Moisture transport inside the solid is driven by diffusion down the moisture gradient, limited by internal diffusivity \(D_{\text{eff}}\).

Surface evaporation Nevap = Kevap (wsurf − weq)

Water escapes the surface into the air, driven by the concentration difference and limited by air velocity coefficient \(K_{\text{evap}}\).

Bottleneck Criteria Bim = Kevap R / Deff

Bi < 0.1: Surface-limited (slow boundary escape). Bi > 10: Bulk-diffusion-limited (surface dries, core remains wet).

Diffusion Time Scale tdiff ∼ R² / Dbulk

The time required for internal moisture to travel out scales quadratically with radius \(R\). Cutting in half reduces drying time by 4x!
