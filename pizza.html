📐 Governing Physics & Kinetics

Arrhenius Cooking Rate Rc = A0·e-Ea / RT·(1 - c)

Pizza cooking is modeled as a chemical reaction with activation energy $E_a$. The pre-exponential factor $A_0$ is dynamically calibrated using the supermarket recipe (A center doneness of 99% is reached after 12 minutes in a preheated oven at 200°C).

Fourier Conduction with Phase Change ρ·Cp(T)·∂T/∂t = k·∂²T/∂x²

Transient heat conduction from the oven boundary. To represent the frozen state, the specific heat capacity $C_p(T)$ peaks sharply at $0^\circ\text{C}$ to account for the latent heat of ice melting ($133.6\text{ kJ/kg}$), creating the temperature plateau.
