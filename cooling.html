Design-Before-Building Laboratory

In chemical engineering, running laboratory test runs with real reactors is expensive and time-consuming. We use numerical simulation to perform parameter sweeps and optimize geometry virtually.

Designing a travel mug is a multi-layer radial transient conduction problem. We must keep the coffee inside hot for a 2-hour lecture (\(>60^\circ\text{C}\)), but prevent the outer casing from getting too hot (\(<50^\circ\text{C}\)) to hold comfortably.

By simulating steel vs. ceramic inner walls, vacuum vs. foam insulation, and lid positions, students learn how different thermal resistances add up in series to dictate total heat flux.
