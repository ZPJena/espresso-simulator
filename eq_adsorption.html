Didactical Background (Technical Chemistry)

The adsorption isotherm describes the thermodynamic equilibrium between the solid-phase adsorbate loading X and the fluid-phase concentration c. In fixed-bed adsorption columns, the mass transfer front (breakthrough front) propagates through the bed at a velocity inversely proportional to the slope of the isotherm. The simulation solves the time-dependent advection-reaction-diffusion PDE to demonstrate breakthrough front sharpening (favorable isotherm) and dispersion.
